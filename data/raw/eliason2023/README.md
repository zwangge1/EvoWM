S1_limb_data.csv - Raw limb data set used in the study
	Taxon - Name of species
	Clade - Avian clade
	Family - Avian family name
	Catnum_or_ref - museum specimen number, or citation used
	fem_leng_mm - Femur length in mm
	tbt_leng_mm - Tibiotarsus length in mm
	tmt_leng_mm - Tarsometatarsus length in mm
	hum_leng_mm - Humerus length in mm
	raduln_leng_mm - Radius/ulna length in mm
	cmc_leng_mm - Carpometacarpus length in mm
	mass_g - Body mass in grams
	Citations - Sources of data
	Comments - Additional remarks
